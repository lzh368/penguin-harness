WikiSkill `2026.10.21.5` distinguishes observed predictions from supported rules throughout Wiki, proposal and audit. Learning roles must trace consequential rules to their premises; neither conflicting fields, coincident conditions nor reproducing a historical answer establishes a general business policy. Candidate bodies retain executable methods and parameter sources, while concrete training outputs stay in PURPOSE.

Auditors check every consequential rule before scenario walkthroughs and return all identified blocking findings together. Pre-stage revision resumes the original learning sessions within the frozen allowance, repairs Wiki and proposal through their respective roles, and rechecks the current content without clearing candidates or bypassing revision accounting. New runs record the allowance before learning, defaulting to one revision of an already submitted proposal; existing runs retain their frozen rules. Preview, staged diff and actual injection checks are distinguished. This changes learning instructions only, not scoring, acceptance, model, split, K or running experiments. Improved scores require subsequent evaluation.
